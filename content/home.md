## hey, I'm Kazys

I’ve been programming for over 20 years. By day I work as a full-stack developer (Go, Kubernetes, web), but that’s only part of the story. Most of my free time ends up circling back to the same thing that got me into programming in the first place — making games, engines, and weird experimental tools just to see if I can.

I’m especially drawn to recreational programming: building game engine architecture, experimenting with ECS designs, procedural generation, rendering techniques, data structures, and low-level systems. I like starting from a blank page and growing something complex out of simple rules, even if it means reinventing the wheel along the way. A lot of my projects live somewhere between “toy” and “research,” usually driven by curiosity rather than any plan to ship.

Programming, to me, is a creative medium as much as a technical one — a playground for exploring ideas, understanding how things work, and occasionally getting lost in a problem that’s interesting enough to exist just for its own sake.

Currently working on: **headless WASM ports of C libraries** and this very site.
